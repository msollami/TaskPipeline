Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 14, 2024

Overview

TaskPipeline ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data.

What We DON'T Collect:

  • No personal information
  • No usage analytics
  • No crash reports
  • No tracking data
  • No cookies or identifiers

Data Storage

All data created in TaskPipeline stays on your device:

  • Task names and durations: Stored locally on your Mac
  • Settings and preferences: Stored locally using standard macOS preferences
  • No cloud sync: Your data never leaves your device

Third-Party Services

TaskPipeline does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, or advertising networks.

Permissions

The app requests minimal macOS permissions:

  • Notifications: To alert you when tasks complete (optional, you can deny this)
  • Menubar access: To display the app in your menubar

Children's Privacy

Since we don't collect any data, TaskPipeline is safe for users of all ages, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect your data, there's nothing for us to access, modify, or delete. You have complete control over your data at all times, as it only exists on your device.

Summary

TaskPipeline is privacy-first by design. We believe your task lists and time management should be completely private. Everything stays on your Mac, under your control.
