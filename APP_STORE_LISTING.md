## Description

Stay focused and manage your time visually with TaskPipeline, a beautiful menu bar timer app designed for productivity.

**KEY FEATURES:**

• Visual Timeline Editor - See your entire day at a glance with color-coded task segments
• Drag & Drop Interface - Easily reorder tasks and adjust durations with intuitive gestures
• Break Reminders - Schedule breaks between tasks with motivational quotes
• Customizable Clock - Personalize colors and sync with your current task
• Pipeline Templates - Save and reuse common task sequences
• Smart Time Locking - Maintain total session duration while adjusting individual tasks
• Minimal & Elegant - Clean menu bar design that stays out of your way

**PERFECT FOR:**
• Pomodoro Technique practitioners
• Time blocking enthusiasts
• Students managing study sessions
• Professionals organizing work sprints
• Anyone wanting better time awareness

**FOCUSED EXPERIENCE:**
When your timer runs, TaskPipeline displays a clean, focused view showing your current task, remaining time, and progress through your session. The app sits quietly in your menu bar until you need it.

**PRIVACY FIRST:**
All your data stays on your Mac. No accounts, no tracking, no cloud sync required.

Start managing your time visually today with TaskPipeline.
